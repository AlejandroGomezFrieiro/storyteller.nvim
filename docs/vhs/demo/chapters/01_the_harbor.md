# Chapter 1 — The Harbor

## Scene 1 — The warning

- **POV:** Odysseus
- **Location:** Ithaca
- **Beat:** A warning arrives too late.

Odysseus watched the harbor lights bend in the rain. A messenger crossed the
stone quay with a sealed letter and no dry place left to hide it.

## Scene 2 — The choice

- **POV:** Odysseus
- **Location:** Ithaca
- **Beat:** Leave before the tide turns.

The letter named the danger plainly. By dawn, the ship would either leave or
be trapped against the shore.
